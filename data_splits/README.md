# data_splits

Lists of image paths **relative to the dataset root** (the `Bone Fractures Detection` folder). They are generated by
`scripts/eda/make_clean_splits.py` from `results/eda/image_groups.csv` and must not be edited by hand.
`scripts/training/run_baseline.py` turns them into absolute-path lists at run time (so the same files work locally and on Kaggle).

## official/
- `train.txt`, `val.txt`, `test.txt`: the original Roboflow split (E1).
- `test_with_train_copy.txt` (48) / `test_no_train_copy.txt` (16): official test images with / without a verified near-duplicate in train (leak probe). Same for `val_*`.

## clean5/ (HBFMID-Clean, E2)
- `fold{k}_{train,val,test}.txt`: fold k tests on group-fold k and validates on group-fold (k+1) mod 5.
- `assignments.csv`: every image with group id, group fold, representative flag and its role in each fold
  (`role_f0..role_f4` in {train, val, test, excluded}).
- `fold_class_groups.csv`: independent groups / images per class, fold and role (input for the group-calibrated loss).
- `summary.json`: parameters, seed, checks, per-fold counts, sha256 of `image_groups.csv`.

Rules: splitting unit = independent group; train keeps every member of its groups; val/test use one representative image per
(group, label-set); the other members of an evaluation group are `excluded` (never used for training in that fold).
Regenerate with:
`python scripts/eda/make_clean_splits.py --groups results/eda/image_groups.csv --out data_splits --data-root "<dataset root>" --k 5 --seed 42`
